Visualizer Proposals

Six clean styles for the top panel, each drawn in two or three theme roles on the theme's background. The large frame is Visualizer only; the small one is Art + visualizer, with the style as a 200-tall band along the foot. The signal is simulated (a kick, a hat, two moving mid peaks, noise).

Theme

Cost guesses are relative to Peaks and are estimates from the filter chain, not measurements: each graph still has to be timed on the handheld. All six are drawn grey by the audio filter and coloured by the palette lookup the theme styles already use, so no per-frame colour conversion is added. Also considered and left out: a VU needle pair (a rotation per frame, costly), a constellation (lines between moving points have no cheap filter) and a heatmap grid (too close to Dots).
