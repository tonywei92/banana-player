Try it

How it behaves

  • Turning it on: a Guided row in the Reading menu (START while reading), kept for the series like the reading mode. It works in One screen and Fullscreen; Book shows whole pages and has no Guided.
  • Panels are found as each page is decoded: the plain white or black gutters between panels split the page. Nothing to download, no special comic files.
  • Order follows the reading mode: right to left reads the top row's right panel first (as manga does); left to right the left.
  • Each panel fills the screen with a small margin, gliding from the last one. In Fullscreen a tall panel uses both screens.
  • A page it can't split (art across the gutters, slanted panels, a single splash) shows whole, as reading does now, and the next press turns the page.
  • After the last panel the next press goes to the next page: a glance at the whole page first (a setting), then its first panel.
  • Touch: a tap on a panel in the bottom map jumps there; a swipe on the top-panel area steps as LEFT/RIGHT do.
