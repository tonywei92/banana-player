Handheld home grid

Home: Recent strip + 2×2 grid

Bottom panel of the handheld, drawn like ui.cpp draws it (the palette, Pixelify Sans, the app's own pixel icons, 640×480 layout units). Try it with the buttons, the keyboard, or by clicking the panel as if it were the touch screen.

Design notes and decisions
  1. Why: the first screen answers “carry on” (Recent) and “where do I go” (four big tiles) without tabs to learn. Files replaces today's Local tab, and Recent moves out of it.
  2. Focus: starts on the newest Recent card (on Files when Recent is empty). UP/DOWN move between the strip and the grid's rows, LEFT/RIGHT along the strip or across columns. Coming back up or down returns to the card or column you left.
  3. Strip: cards 240 wide, two and a half in view. The focused card stays in the second slot so the cards on either side always show. The last card is All recent, which opens the full 30-item list. Swipe it by touch, hold a card for its menu.
  4. Empty Recent: the strip shrinks to one hint line and the tiles grow taller. Nothing has to be explained twice.
  5. Inside a section: the header reads “‹ Files” with four section markers. L/R jump to the previous or next section, which replaces today's L/R tabs. B at a section's top goes Home with that tile focused.
  6. Y on a card: Resume, Play from the start, Show in folder, Add to playlist…, Remove from Recent. X on Home: Clear Recent (press twice), Show hidden folders, Settings.
  7. START menu: drop its Recent tab, leaving Settings · About · Quit. Recent then lives in one place. From a video, B×2 lands on its list, and B from a section's top goes Home.
  8. B on Home: “Press B again to quit”, as today. Extensions: a placeholder that says what's coming in general terms and names no service.
  9. For ui.cpp: the strip's scroll eases over about 120 ms, so it needs a tick while it moves. Everything else redraws on signature() as today.
