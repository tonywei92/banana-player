Play On Picker

Play on: choose where it plays, from the handheld

A mock for approval. Today the handheld can only take the sound back (hold A); a dashboard page has to send it to itself. This adds the other way: the handheld picks the output, Handheld or any connected browser.

1. START › Playing gets a Play on row

Shown only while at least one dashboard page that can play is open. Its note says where it plays now. A opens the picker.

Bottom panel · START › Playing
No browser connected: the row isn't there (as today)

2. The picker

The handheld first, then each open page by its browser and device ("Chrome on Mac", "Safari on iPhone"; a second one of the same kind gets "2"). The one playing now is ticked. Up/Down move, A picks, B closes. Picking moves the file, its place, and the audio and subtitle choice, as the dashboard's switch does.

Bottom panel · Play on
Top panel, unchanged behind it

3. After picking Chrome on Mac

The picker closes on the playing screen. The top panel names where it plays. A plays and pauses the browser, hold A brings it back here, as today.

Top panel
Bottom panel

4. When the browser won't start sound by itself

Safari, and Chrome on a page nobody has tapped yet, refuse to start sound that a tap didn't ask for. The page then shows one big button; the handheld says it's waiting. B on the handheld (or Handheld in the picker) gives up and plays here instead.

Top panel, waiting
The phone's dashboard

Rules

  • Which pages are listed: signed-in dashboard pages that are open now and able to play (pages hidden in a background tab count; a page closed for 10 s drops off, as the Session's tab rule).
  • Names come from the browser's own description (no setting to type); unknown ones read "Browser".
  • Photos stay on the handheld: the row isn't shown while a photo is open.
  • Nothing else moves the output: as today, only an explicit choice (this picker, the dashboard's switch, hold A).
  • Quick settings (F) could later get the same row; START only for now.
