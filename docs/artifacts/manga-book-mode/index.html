Book mode

Hold the RG DS sideways like a book: one page on each screen, the D-pad's sideways press turns the spread. Click the keys on the drawing (or press ← → on your keyboard) to try it.

How it behaves

  • Turning it on: the Reading menu (START) gets a Book row: Off, Top screen left, Top screen right. SELECT stays Fullscreen. Book is kept for the series, like the reading mode.
  • One page a screen, turned a quarter turn and fitted whole. Both screens show their page at once, so a spread reads like an open book.
  • The order follows the reading mode. Right to left: the earlier page on the right. Left to right: on the left. A series in Strip opens Book in Right to left.
  • By spread: a sideways press turns two pages. L / R move one, for a cover on its own or a two-page picture that landed split.
  • The pages and their numbers turn. The page numbers show for a moment after a turn, at the foot of the spread, upright as you hold it. Everything else on the bottom screen (the Reading menu, notices) is drawn as always, in the screen's own orientation: held sideways it reads sideways, and you turn the handheld back to use the menu.
  • Touch: drag sideways on the bottom screen to turn; a tap shows the page numbers. No zoom in Book (the D-pad's other axis does nothing).
  • Leaving: B leaves Book (as it leaves Fullscreen), then closes the reader. When Book is turned on, a notice on both screens says which way to turn the handheld.
