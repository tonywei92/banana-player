Ten Fun Visualizers

Ten playful styles for the top panel: small characters and objects that move to the song. Each is flat shapes in two or three theme roles on the theme's background, so it recolours with all 17 themes; try Light and Sakura. The large frame is Visualizer only. The small one is Art + visualizer: six styles sit in the 200-tall band under the cover, four fill the panel behind it. The signal is simulated (a kick, a hat, two moving mid peaks, noise) and swells and quietens every few seconds so the quiet behaviour shows too.

Theme

Cost guesses are relative to Peaks and are estimates from vertex counts and blended area, not measurements: each style still has to be timed on the handheld. All ten read the band levels, loudness and beat hint the worker already produces. Every character is a handful of flat shapes or a 1-bit sprite tinted by a role: no fixed-colour art. Considered and left out: a tape deck and a heartbeat monitor (both close to meters already rejected), a lava lamp (needs a per-pixel shader to look right), a typewriter (reads as text, not music).
