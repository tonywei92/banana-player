Manga Reader Mock

Manga reader · screens

A proposal, nothing built. Each screen is both panels of the handheld, drawn on the bottom panel's 640×480 layout with the Midnight palette, Pixelify Sans and the app's own glyphs. The extension is off by default; every screen here exists only once Manga reader is turned on in Manage extensions. Titles, authors, sources and repositories are invented; covers are generated (gradient plus a motif), and the page art is drawn shapes. The design text is docs/design/manga.md.
