Design notes

What this proposes

  • Home is the first tab and the landing page (phone tab bar, tablet rail, desktop sidebar: Home, Playing, Files, Playlists, Manga). The dashboard opens on it; #/recent goes to it. It needs one new glyph, a pixel house (drawn here; it would be a new G_* glyph for the sprite).
  • Recent leaves Playlists: the pinned Recent card on the Playlists page and the Recent line in the desktop sidebar both go (tap Playlists in the mock to see the page without it). Liked stays pinned.
  • Two parts only: Continue, then Recent. The newest item is one large card with a single yellow action (Resume at 4:12 / Play / Open on handheld), then From the start, Like and the menu. Everything older is one collection with filters (All, Videos, Music, Books) and a Grid or List view, remembered by the browser. Grid is the default on tablet and desktop, List on a phone. The first 8 show (6 on a phone), then "Show all".
  • Kinds read at a glance by the cover's shape: a video fills the frame, a song is a square sleeve, a book, PDF or comic is an upright page. All are generated gradients (the real page would put the file's own cover over them, as elsewhere).
  • Progress in the handheld's words: 4:12 / 9:56 with a yellow bar, Played, Page 7 of 18, 37% read, Finished. Songs say Play, not Resume, since songs start from the beginning.
  • Honest about where things can go. Books, PDFs and comics carry "Opens on handheld" and their action is Open on handheld. Network files carry "Network" and always play on the handheld, whatever Play on says; "Play in this browser" is greyed in their menu with the reason. A file gone from the card is dimmed, marked Gone, and can only be removed.
  • Play on (Handheld / This browser) sits in Home's header. It is the same setting as the player's device button, shown where a first-time user decides. Resume labels follow it.
  • What's playing isn't a second player. The playing file is the newest Recent item, so the Continue card turns into Now playing (live time, Open player, Pause) rather than a separate block; the mini player and the player bar stay as they are. Switch "Playing" above to see it.
  • Quick actions under Recent are actions, not a second nav: Shuffle Liked, Upload files, New playlist.
  • Empty: a welcome with the handheld-and-browser picture, two actions (Browse Files, Upload files), four ways to start (network folders marked as set up on the handheld, since the browser can't browse them yet), three facts on how the two devices relate, and dashed placeholders showing what will appear.
  • History off: one yellow-edged banner saying why Home is empty and where the setting is (Settings › Files › Keep play history), then the same ways to start. Now playing still shows when something plays.
  • Row menu (a sheet on a phone, a popover by the button elsewhere; right-click on a computer in the real page): Resume on handheld, Resume in this browser, Play from the start, Like, Add to playlist, Show in Files, Remove from Recent (with Undo). Header ⋯: Clear Recent.

What it needs from the server (not in /api/recent today)

  • Today's /api/recent lists songs and videos on the card only. This design also lists books, PDFs and comics (kind, page / pages or percent) and network files (flagged), as the handheld's Home does.
  • Open on handheld for a document is a new action: the browser asks the handheld to open that book at its place.
  • Play from the start is new in the browser (play at 0).

For the owner to choose

  • Documents from the browser: build "Open on handheld" (as shown), or list them as read-only cards that only say where you are?
  • Network files on Home: list them (handheld only, as shown), or keep leaving them out of the browser's Recent as today?
  • Now playing on a phone: the Continue card and the mini player both show the playing file. Keep both (as shown), or hide the mini player while that card is on screen?
  • History off: instructions only (as shown), or a "Turn on" button in the dashboard as well?
  • Landing page: always Home, or still Playing when something is already playing as the page opens?
  • Quick actions: keep the row, or leave Home as Continue and Recent only?
  • Incognito: while it's on, should Home say "Incognito is on: what plays now isn't kept" above Recent?
