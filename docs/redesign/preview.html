Proposal · 15 Nov 2026 · measured against 0.7.18 · nothing here is built

Banana Player on the handheld: room for Books, and one way to do everything

Home’s four tiles are full, so a fifth section has nowhere to go. Fixing that properly means fixing what sits under it: the same key does a different job on almost every screen. This proposal replaces the tiles with a rail, gives each key one job, and puts the top screen to work.

1

A rail instead of four tiles

Sections sit down the left edge of the bottom screen, named, 68 × 51 each. L / R still step them. Six today with Books, room for eight, then it scrolls.

2

Books is a section

A shelf of covers for EPUB, PDF, TXT and comics, the ones you are reading first. Manga moves beside it instead of hiding under Extensions.

3

One job per key

X More, Y List, SELECT View, START Menu, F Quick settings: on every screen, with a touch key in the same place.

4

A player in three tiers

Big transport, labelled state chips, and one sheet for the rest, the same for a video and a song. The seek gets on-screen keys.

5

The top screen earns its keep

With nothing playing it previews what the cursor is on: a file’s tracks, a book’s cover and the line you stopped at, a setting’s live effect.

1 · What is wrong today

Six problems, from the screens as they are

Shots are the desktop build of 0.7.18 with generated media (the Big Buck Bunny trailer, test clips, a generated text file), driven by the --press harness.

The navigation is a fixed four

Home is a 2 × 2 grid under the Recent strip, and inside a section the same four are unlabelled 36-unit markers beside the clock. Neither has a fifth slot. Books would have to push something out or shrink every tile.

Books have no home; comics are three levels down

EPUB, PDF and TXT open only from wherever they lie in Files. Comics need Home › Extensions › Manga reader. “Extensions” spends a top-level slot on a drawer that holds one tile.

A key means something else on every screen

SELECT is Now playing, Tracks, Display, Fit / Fill, Book mode, Fullscreen, EQ on / off or Select all, depending where you stand. X and START drift the same way (table below). Nothing learned on one screen carries to the next.

Two menus per list, three kinds of menu

In Files, X is “Options” and Y is “Menu”, and Info is in both. Elsewhere options are a pop-up, a full page (Text options), a page with tabs (Tracks), a grid pop-up (Display) or a panel (Quick settings).

The player is sixteen keys of one weight

Ten 108 × 60 keys, two tappable lines and four more keys below. Pause is the size of Menu. The key the hand needs most on a touch screen, seek, has no button. Loop and Shuffle share one key with five readings: Off, All, One, On, Once.

The top screen idles; labels overflow

On every browsing screen with nothing playing, the top panel is the logo and one line. Meanwhile the bottom one is short of room: the reader’s “Bookmarks” and “Book mode” labels touch their keys’ edges, and the hint line is the smallest type on the device.

2 · Principles

Seven rules the screens below follow

A key keeps its job

If X is More here, X is More there. Where a screen has nothing for a key, the key does nothing rather than something else.

Every key has a touch twin, in the same spot

‹ top left is B. ⋯ top right is X. The row above the hints holds Y and SELECT with their names on them. No function is buttons-only or touch-only.

One sheet for every menu

More, List, View, Menu and Quick settings are the same piece: it rises over the bottom screen, the top keeps its picture, and the key that opened it (or B) closes it.

Size follows use

What is pressed every minute is the biggest thing on the screen. What is set once a film is a labelled chip. What is rare is in a sheet. A free-standing key is at least 56 units on its short side (7.1 mm on the 4-inch RG DS, 7.6 mm on the Plus); list rows are 52.

State is written down

The current subtitle, speed, repeat mode, EQ preset and reading colours are words on the screen, on the control that changes them.

Away from normal is always in sight

Anything changed from its normal state that alters what you see or hear shows in yellow, as its value, on its own control, and is echoed (mini bar, header) on screens that don’t hold the control. Normal is quiet: no badge means nothing is changed. The table.

Two screens, two jobs

The bottom is for the hand. The top is for the eyes: the picture when something plays, and otherwise the detail of whatever the cursor is on.

3 · Information architecture

The new map

Three kinds of screen, and five sheets that work on all of them.

The shellHas the rail. Home and the sections: lists, grids and Settings. L R step sections; L2 R2 step tabs inside one. The mini bar sits above the hints while something plays.
The playerNo rail: the whole bottom screen is controls. One layout for video and music. B leaves it playing and returns you to where you were.
The viewersPhoto, book, PDF, comic. No rail. One skeleton: the thing to drag, the hand’s keys, and a row of List · View · Light.
The sheetsX More · Y List · SELECT View · START Menu · F Quick settings. Each opens over any of the three above.

4c · RG DS and RG DS Plus

Does it work on both? Yes, after one pass of fixes

Both are 4:3 and the bottom panel is laid out in 640 × 480 units, so the two show the same layout. The RG DS is 4.0 in (81.3 × 61.0 mm, 0.127 mm a unit, one unit = one pixel); the Plus is 4.3 in (87.4 × 65.5 mm, 0.137 mm a unit, 1.6 pixels a unit). Everything is 7% smaller in the hand on the RG DS, so that is the one to design for. The first version of these screens used a 44-unit floor, which is 5.6 mm there: too small. This version is resized; use the switch above the walk-through to see either device at its real size.

4d · Power & screens

When a screen may turn off, and when the handheld may sleep

Three states, and who owns each. Both on. Screen off: the player turns the bottom panel’s light off (and, for a song, optionally the top one’s); everything keeps running; the player owns it. Sleep: BananaOS stops the player and darkens both screens; BananaOS owns it, the lid triggers it. The one rule behind the table: a screen that is showing you something stays on; the handheld sleeps only when nothing would be lost or interrupted.

Needs checking with BananaOS

Every “Yes” in the last column assumes BananaOS has an idle sleep of its own and a way for a program to hold it off (an inhibit), or has no idle sleep at all. This repo’s notes say only that bananad stops the player on sleep and that the lid triggers it. If there is no idle sleep, those rows already hold. If there is one and no way to hold it off, they cannot be kept, and jobs and long films will be cut by it, as today.

The lid shut: playing, the keys, incognito

Since this was drawn the app has got “When the lid closes: Pause / Keep playing” (a song or a video goes on), and the power key is becoming play / pause with the lid shut. Two things are added here. The keys with the lid shut: volume keys press for volume and hold for next / previous (the player owns them while shut, so the volume never climbs during a hold), the handheld does not sleep while something is loaded, the shoulder keys are a setting, everything under the lid is ignored (Lid shut: the keys). Incognito: closing the lid, or going to sleep, can close all and leave incognito, and the Web dashboard is off while incognito is on (Incognito: the lid and sleep, the Web dashboard is off). The rules in full: lid-and-incognito.md.

What changes from today

A five-second dimmed warning before an automatic Screen off. Reading holds the handheld awake for ten minutes after the last page. Jobs hold it awake until done, with an “Awake” badge. A browser playing lets both screens go dark. Low battery shortens timers and, at 5%, pauses with your place kept. Everything else is 0.7.18’s behaviour, written down.

8 · For the owner

Open questions

The owner has answered these (15 Nov). Three went another way than first assumed: comics do show in Books, UP / DOWN are no longer speed, and picture looks are global with no CRT. The rest stand as proposed.

Rail, or a bigger grid of tiles?

The rail costs 78 of 640 units on every shell screen and keeps all sections one press away. The alternative keeps Home as tiles (3 × 2) and shows no sections inside a list.

Decided, as proposed: the rail.

Open · Does the Web dashboard follow the handheld’s theme?

Proposed: yes. One Theme setting, shown on the handheld and on the dashboard’s Settings page; a page takes the handheld’s theme with its state. The other way is a theme per browser, kept in that browser, which lets a phone stay dark while the handheld is Light, at the cost of a second setting and two places that can disagree.

Proposed: it follows the handheld.

Open · Are the light-bodied themes worth their cost?

Light, Sakura, 16-bit, 16-bit Lavender and 32-bit are the themes whose ground is not dark, so they are the ones that needed rules: a paper veil under sheets, a dark accent with light ink, and cards over a picture that stay dark. They are also the ones that read best in sunlight. The other way is to ship the dark themes first and add the light-bodied ones once one has been tried outdoors on the handheld.

Proposed: keep them in the set; try one on the handheld before the release that carries them.

What fills the Books shelf?

A Books folder per card (as Manga and videos work) plus anything opened from Files or Network. The other way is scanning the whole card for EPUB / PDF / TXT, which is slower and catches manuals and notes.

Decided, as proposed: the folder, plus what you open.

Do comics (CBZ, CBR) show in Books too?

They are listed in Books as well as in Manga, and open in the same comic reader with the same place.

Decided by the owner: yes.

Extensions leave the top level?

An extension that is on gets its own rail slot; on / off moves to Settings › Extensions, and Manga settings appear in Settings as well as in Manga’s X. This reverses “Manga settings are not in Banana Player’s Settings”.

Decided, as proposed: yes to both.

Is losing X = Loop / Shuffle on the player acceptable?

X becomes More everywhere, so the play mode takes two presses (Y, then the switch) or a tap on its chip. If one-press matters, hold X could cycle it.

Decided, as proposed: two presses is fine.

Stop, Lock and Screen off behind X?

They leave the player’s face for the first row of More: one press more by button, two taps by touch. Screen off also joins Quick settings. Stop could instead keep a small red key by the Next line. Since then Stop has come back to the player’s face, in the place of the View key (the chips open View); it stays in More for the keys.

Decided, as proposed: in More.

START as Menu instead of Playing / Settings / About?

People who press START then A to Like will find places instead. L3 and the Like key cover it, but it is the biggest habit this proposal breaks.

Decided, as proposed: yes.

Drop the “..” row?

‹ and B both go up, and the path is in the header. The row costs one of seven or eight rows in every folder.

Decided, as proposed: dropped.

How rich may the top-screen preview be?

Text and a cover are cheap. A poster frame from a video means opening it in a second decoder on a CPU that is already the limit. The screens assume metadata always, pictures only when one already exists (cover.jpg, the photo, a book’s cover).

Decided, as proposed: no video frame grabs.

Inter for small text?

Notes, hints and paths are set in Inter at 11.5 to 13 units; names, titles and keys stay in the pixel face. It reads better small, and moves a little away from the all-pixel look.

Decided, as proposed: yes, for secondary text only.

Should UP / DOWN stay speed?

No. UP and DOWN do nothing on the player, so a stray press changes nothing. Speed is set in View › Speed: SELECT, or a tap on the Speed chip. The chips take no focus from the D-pad.

Decided by the owner: not speed. UP and DOWN do nothing on the player; the chips are touch only, and SELECT opens View by the keys.

Picture looks: which, and how they are kept?

Sharpen, Soften, Scanlines, LCD grid, Retro, with Strength. A look is global: it stays on across files; “Only this video” is opt-in. No CRT look. A look that is on is always said: the Picture chip, a top-screen note when a video starts, the mini bar.

Decided by the owner: global by default, no CRT, always shown.

Ask before Manga stops a video?

The sheet costs one press each time. “Don’t ask again” brings back today’s behaviour for those who want it. The other way is no sheet and a louder notice. Also: with no engine installed (card manga only), must a video still stop, or could it just pause while the pages have the top screen?

Decided, as proposed: ask, Stay focused, with “Don’t ask again”; a video still stops in Manga either way.

How loud should “not normal” be?

Drawn: yellow value and a dot on the control; pills on the mini bar for what plays; header badges only for Incognito and volume past 100%; nothing left on the top screen. More would be a permanent top-screen mark while browsing; less would be the control only.

Decided, as proposed: as drawn; the top screen stays clean.

Adjust: Gamma in, Hue out? One “Use for” for everything?

Gamma is the slider that rescues a dark film; Hue mostly breaks things. Size, look and Adjust share one “All videos / Only this video” choice, so a file is remembered whole. Separate scopes are possible but need three switches.

Decided, as proposed: four sliders, one scope.

7 mm everywhere, or seven rows?

Free-standing keys are now 7 mm or more on the 4-inch RG DS. Rows in sheets, list rows and the visualizer’s style tiles are 5.6 to 6.6 mm tall. Taking them to 56 units costs a row per screen, scrolling in the More sheet and paging in the style grid. Also: should the RG DS default PDFs to Width fit and books to a larger text size?

Decided, as proposed: rows stay as drawn (they are wide, and the D-pad reaches them); device-specific defaults: yes.

In what order to build it?

8b · For the build

Components

Every piece that appears on more than one of the screens, so each is coded once. Sizes are in the panel’s 640 × 480 units. Each says which existing building block it maps to: REUSE exists, use as is · VARIANT exists, needs a variant · NEW.

Themes

A theme is a set of values for the tokens of src/theme.h, and nothing else: every component above is drawn with those tokens, so no component and no screen has a colour or a rule of its own for a theme. Sizes, corners, type, layouts, states and the focus rule are the same in all seventeen. The Theme switch at the foot of this page sets the same variables the code will swap; Settings › Power & screens › Theme is where it is changed on the handheld. Cards over a video, a photo or album art take the TOP_ tokens, which are dark in every theme. The values, the contrast of each pair, what stays fixed, and the table of every component with the tokens it uses are in components.md, section Themes; themes.py works the values and ratios out.

What was drawn two ways: now one

Seventeen places where two screens showed nearly the same thing with different sizes or behaviour. Every screen has been redrawn to the one version; the screens and the examples above are drawn by the same functions and style rules, so they cannot drift apart. The few deliberate exceptions are named.

What this preview has not checked

The real panels

These are HTML drawings at the panel’s units, not the handheld. The real-size switch depends on your screen reporting its size truthfully (hence the 50 mm rule to calibrate), and a browser’s smooth text is kinder than SDL_ttf at 12 px. Legibility of 12-unit Inter, the rail under a left thumb and the reach to ⋯ need the device.

Drawing cost

The top-screen previews assume the top panel can show a laid-out card with a picture while idle. Today it is an mpv overlay; how far that stretches is an engineering question.

See Coverage above: what an audit of today’s app found is now drawn or listed there with the reason.
