## Match album art

`artbg::artBackdrop(rgba, w, h)` does the spec's pipeline, pure: a 32 x 32 copy median-cut into 8 colours; near-black,
near-white, >85% saturation, <5% share and greys (under 10% saturation, which have no hue to give) dropped; the
largest left gives the hue. The picture is the art cropped to 4:3, box-filtered to 160 x 120, a real Gaussian blur
(sigma 10 px, 61 taps, edges clamped) and saturation x0.55; the two 95th-percentile luminances are the rows above and
below y 168 on the panel.

`artbg::backdropFor(themeColours, hue, l95top, l95low)` gives the tinted charcoal ground (S 15% / L 13%, blended 45%
into half way BG -> SURFACE), the five stops (66 / 60 / 80 / 90 / 94, deepened until TEXT holds 4.5:1 above y 168 and
DIM_ON 4.5:1 below), or the neutral fallback (the charcoal 35% towards SURFACE, down to BG) when there is no hue, when
every stop would pass 96%, or when a theme accent with a colour of its own is 150..210 degrees from the art's hue.

The cache is `artbg/<fnv64>.bg`: `fnv64` over the encoded art's bytes, then a 5-byte header (version, hue or 0xFFFF,
the two luminances) and 160 x 120 RGB. `encode`/`decode`, `cacheName`/`cachePath` and `evict` (oldest `used` first,
16 MB cap) are pure. No worker, no threads, no SDL and no page wiring: that is the next step.
