# Home's real album art (2026-11-19)

Home's Continue and Recent cards drew a song as `Kit::artPlaceholder` forever: the record was the picture even when
the file had art. They now show the song's real cover, decoded off the main thread, and the Now playing card is named
by the song's tag title (the mini bar's "Song 2", not the file's "song2").

## What changed

- `src/coverworker.h` / `.cpp`: `CoverWorker::wantSong(key, path, w, h)`, the same worker Manga's covers use. Its
  source is resolved with `probeCover` (`src/web/probe.cpp`), so it is the attached picture in the file, else
  `cover.*` / `folder.*` / `front.*` beside it, and decoded with `decodePage`. A song is cropped to the card's aspect
  from the **middle** (album art is a sleeve, not a page whose top holds the title); Manga's covers keep their top
  rows as before. The existing "decode wider when the picture is too short for the box" pass applies to both.
- `src/main.cpp`: one worker still serves both. The `evCover` branch takes its results and splits them by key:
  `home:` keys go to `ui.browse->homeView->coversReady`, everything else to `ui.manga->coversReady`. Background
  threads still only push the SDL event; textures are made on the main thread.
- `src/ui_home.h` / `.cpp`: Home keeps a small LRU of textures (`ART_KEEP` 12), asks once per key, and keeps a
  per-path failure set so a song with no art (or an unreadable one) keeps its placeholder and is never asked again.
  `drawSongArt` draws the placeholder first (its edge and rounded corners stay whole), then copies the texture into
  the picture's box inset by `theme::EDGE`. The key includes the card's pixel box and radius because a texture is
  cropped and rounded for one box: the Continue card's 128×84 crop cannot also be the Recent card's 104×48 strip.
  Corners are rounded at upload in the texture's own alpha; the rounding (`roundCorners`) moved from ui_manga.cpp's
  private cover helper into `ui_internal.h`, so Home and Manga share it. `Home::sig` carries `artSerial`, so an
  arriving cover redraws the page.
- `CoverWorker::cancel` (Manga reopened) now drops only Manga's queued requests; Home's `home:` requests are not
  Manga's to cancel.
- `src/ui_home.cpp`, `continueOf`: for a playing song, the card's title is `p.title` (the tag's) when mpv has one,
  with the Recent name as the fallback.
- `FEATURES.md`: Home's Continue and Recent lines mention album art and the tag title.

Video, photo and book cards are untouched: they keep their kind mark, with no thumbnails.

## Why one worker, one queue

A second worker would decode two pictures at once and could starve a manga grid behind Home's four cards, while a
handheld has one software decoder budget. One queue preserves the existing newest-first behaviour and the 200-item
cap. Results are routed by key rather than by which worker they came from: Home's key prefix is the only contract
between the two areas, and neither touches the other's textures.
