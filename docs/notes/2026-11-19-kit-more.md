## Match album art: the light half (components.md, added 2026-11-19)

`artbg::artBackdrop` now measures four luminances: the 95th and the 5th percentile of the picture's relative
luminance over the two bands of the panel (above and below y 168). The 95th says how deep a dark theme's veil
must be to keep its light text legible over a bright art; the 5th says how white a light theme's must be over a
dark one. The cache header grew to hold them (version 2: 7 bytes — version, the hue or none, the four numbers).

`artbg::backdropFor` picks the branch by the theme itself: a light-bodied theme is one whose BG is lighter than
its TEXT (Light, Sakura, Gundam, the 16-bit ones — every theme in `theme.cpp`'s table divides cleanly that way).
The light version: the paper (half way BG to SURFACE) with the hue at S 22% / L 86% blended in at 45%; the same
five stops, each scaled until the picture's 5th-percentile luminance under it leaves the theme's TEXT, DIM_ON
and ACCENT at 4.5:1 (a dark art is whitened); the fallback — no hue, a clash with a coloured accent (150..210
degrees), or a veil that could not rise far enough — is the theme's plain BG, drawn as the gradient's two ends
in one colour. Tests: `testArtBackdropTheme` (the light palette's own values, a pathological near-grey theme for
the impossible veil), `testArtBackdropCache` (the 7-byte header), `testArtBackdrop` (the 5th percentiles).
