# Quick settings sliders could not be dragged (2026-11-20)

Cause: the one shared slider (`slideGrab`, `src/slider.h`, docs/notes/sliders.md) already drives Quick settings'
brightness and volume, the player's seek bar, the EQ, the readers and the Brightness sheet. Quick settings became an
`Overlay` ("quick") but `Ui::quickSlide` grabbed without `owner`, so on the first move `slideStale()` saw a top
overlay that wasn't the grabber's and dropped the slider: a tap worked (the lift is handled elsewhere), a drag never.
The player's seek has no overlay over it, hence it worked.

Fix: `quickSlide` passes `findOverlay("quick")` as `owner` (as `LightSheet` passes `this`). Volume still goes through
`setVolumeTotal`, brightness through `setBrightness`; nothing new. No second slider was written: there is one, and a
second kit one would have been the duplicate. A new slider must pass its overlay as `owner`.

Check: `--press "F,down:300:200,move:330:200,move:380:200,up:380:200"` with `BP_BACKLIGHT_DIR` on fake sysfs: brightness
128 -> 204 (was unchanged before).
